# How to Handle Agitation and Aggression

This is a draft foundational article for the Project Lantern platform. The content explores understanding the root causes of behavioral changes and learning how to de-escalate difficult situations calmly.

## Introduction

Dementia is a syndrome characterized by a deterioration in memory, thinking, behavior and the ability to perform everyday activities. Although dementia mainly affects older people, it is not a normal part of ageing. Worldwide, around 50 million people have dementia, and there are nearly 10 million new cases every year.

## Key Points to Consider

1. **Medical Consultation:** Always consult with a board-certified neurologist or geriatrician before making care decisions.
2. **Support Systems:** Look into joining local support groups through organizations like the Alzheimer's and Related Disorders Society of India (ARDSI).
3. **Patience and Empathy:** Caregiving requires immense emotional resilience.

### Practical Steps

For families navigating this condition in India, understanding the trajectory of the disease is the first step toward effective caregiving. The lack of specialized memory clinics in tier-2 and tier-3 cities often delays diagnosis.
